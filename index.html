    An artist who builds rooms where people open up.

    I'm a painter and muralist who also dances and writes songs. But what I love most is a moment that happens in almost every session: someone who “can't draw” forgets to be careful, laughs, makes a mess — and suddenly shows up as themselves.

    “

    For the past year I've been researching one question: what happens when creativity becomes a way to communicate — a space where you meet yourself, and really meet the people around you?

    where it began

    It began when I moved from Baku to Barcelona and had to start over in a new language. I drew strangers on the street and learned that real contact often happens before words. Since then, wherever I go, I carry a case of paints. I set it out on a table, and people come by themselves.

    how the room was born

    The Living Room was born from that year of sessions and experiments — Ugly Breakfast, Wear Your Mess, Blind Portraits, Bad Idea Lunch — shared with more than 80 people, from surf camps to founder retreats. Giving people room to create and be themselves has always felt like my calling. Now it's a space you can step into.
